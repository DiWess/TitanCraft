`studio/skills/windows_playtest_journey.md`. Two independent lanes:

1. **Windows runner** — workflow `windows-playtest-journey` built, tested and exported the

2. **Walked playthrough** — `tools/visual_review/playthrough_journey.gd` played the game from

mission logic; it has never shown that the journey can be walked, or run the real victory
transition. Both gaps hid defects reported below.

### Findings

Severity follows `CLAUDE.md` §5. Each is reproduced in both valid takes unless stated.

1. **MAJOR — The beacon activation, the game's climax, never plays.** Activating the beacon
   throws `System.NullReferenceException` at `Beacon.AddExtractionTrauma()`
   (`src/World/Beacon.cs:119`), reached through the real input path
   (`FirstPersonController._UnhandledInput` → `TryInteract` → `Beacon.Interact`). Cause:
   `mission.TryCompleteBeaconActivation()` moves the mission to `Victory`, which synchronously
   fires `CrashSiteEndScreenNavigator.OnMissionChanged` → `ChangeSceneToFile`. Godot 4 removes
   the current scene from the tree immediately, so the rest of `ActivateExtraction()` runs on a
   node with no tree: `GetTree()` is null, the camera shake throws, and the activation sound on
   the next line never plays. The beam never renders either. The frame captured at that moment
   is a single flat colour (standard deviation 0.0), and the next thing the player sees is the
   victory screen. The integration suite cannot catch this because it disables scene changes
   in every scenario.
2. **MAJOR — Onboarding skips the collect step and gives a wrong instruction.** The first
   resource picked up jumps onboarding from "Press Space to jump" straight to "Return to the
   workbench and press E to build the Mechanical Arm Mk I", while the player holds 1 of 3
   resources and the HUD objective line correctly still says to collect all three. The
   "Find Metal, Biomass and Electronics…" prompt is never shown. Cause:
   `CrashSiteHudBinder.UpdateInventory` reports collection when the inventory total is above
   zero, and `OnboardingTutorialState.ReportResourceCollected` then sets the Craft step. This
   defect was introduced by PR #134.
3. **MINOR — The onboarding prompt overlaps the HUD panel.** The top-centre prompt runs across
   the panel's right edge and overdraws its text in every early-game frame (00–07).
4. **MINOR — Internal node names are shown to the player.** "Press E to interact with
   GalaxabrainComponentPickup", "…with SavePoint" (`FirstPersonController.cs:442` builds the
   prompt from `node.Name` minus `Placeholder_`).
5. **MINOR — The startup hint reads as an error.** "Left click: Mk I not built yet — craft it
   at the workbench first." is shown from the first frame, before any attack, and stays for the
   whole collection phase. It is deliberate (`CrashSiteHud.cs:34`); the wording is the problem.
6. **MINOR — The route from the fight to the save point is not direct.** The level generator
   checked that leg from where the component is authored (the Scout's home, 20, −16). The Scout
   chases the player, so it dies elsewhere; in take 4 the straight line from its death point
   (13.8, −13.1) runs into a debris block near (2.6, −11.3). A visible gap lets a player walk
   round it, but a straight walker stalls. Take 1 walked the same leg cleanly.
7. **Not reproduced — not a finding.** The save point needing three attempts (take 1 only), and
   a take-1 fight frame showing the camera inside an enclosed volume with the Scout hidden
   (take 4's fight frame shows the Scout clearly). One hypothesis was tested and rejected: that
   the Scout's hidden component pickup absorbs strikes — the attack ray does not collide with
   areas.

Diagnosis:

- **Focal point — holds through play.** The lit tower beacon beam reads as the landmark from
  spawn; the Scout is the unambiguous focal point of the fight, a long-legged dark silhouette
  framed by market-stall posts against pale walls (`08_fight_first_swing.png`).
- **Silhouette — strong for the enemy, weak for obstacles.** The Scout reads instantly. The
  crash-debris blocks read as flat dark masses with no edge definition
  (`12_stuck_component_to_save_point.png`), which is part of why the post-fight route is unclear.
- **Route readability — carried by the HUD, not the ground.** The walkable ground is a broad
  near-uniform mauve with no path marking visible at eye height; the player navigates by the
  objective text and the beam, not by reading the street.
- **Material coherence — lime render clips.** White lime-washed walls clip to flat white in
  direct light across the district frames, losing the edge and plaster detail the art pass
  built.
- **Scale — correct.** Doorways, stalls and the Scout sit at believable size against the
  first-person camera height.
- **The ending fails outright.** The beacon activation frame is blank
  (`15_leg_10_save_point_to_beacon.png`), and the victory screen is small grey text on near
  black with no image (`16_99_end_screen.png`) — the game's payoff is its weakest moment.
- **HUD overlap** is visible in every early frame (`02_leg_01_spawn_to_metal.png` also shows
  finding 2 directly: the build prompt on screen beside "Biomass 0/3 | Electronics 0/2").

Verdict: `NOT_GO` until the beacon activation renders (finding 1) and the onboarding prompt no
longer overlaps the HUD (finding 3). The exploration and combat frames on their own would hold.

## Final Verdict

Verdict: `FAIL_REPO_OWNED`

The game can be played from spawn to victory on real input, and the Windows build exports and
runs cleanly. It fails on two repository-owned defects the player meets directly: the beacon
activation never plays (finding 1) and onboarding tells the player to build before they can
(finding 2). Both are fixable in the repository without scope change.

Scope note: this document gates the Windows playtest journey only. It does not approve Stage B,
marketing claims, or scene integration of reviewed assets.
