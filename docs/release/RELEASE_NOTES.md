# TitanCraft: Crash Site 1.0.0

The first public release: one scene, one player, one experience.

**What it is.** A short single-player first-person game. Your ship crashed
into the Mwezi Quarter, an abandoned coral-stone harbour quarter held by the
Galaxabrains. Scavenge, build a Mechanical Arm that grows through three
modules, and light the harbour beacon.

**In this release**
- Three arm modules on one arm: Mk I strike; Mk II magnet clamp (pull wreckage
  and drones); Mk III breaker coil (hold to charge, break guards and stone).
- Three Galaxabrains: the Scout, the swarm drone, and the guarded Warden.
- Two gated courts, the Net-makers' Yard and the tower court, and a beacon
  holdout to finish.
- Six optional discoveries that tell the quarter's story.
- Checkpoints at the save point, at every craft and at the power core.
- Settings: sensitivity, invert look, field of view, volume, fullscreen,
  VSync, reduced camera shake, key rebinding.
- Fully offline. No account, no telemetry.

**Download.** `TitanCraft-1.0.0-windows-x64.zip`. Unzip and run
`TitanCraft.exe` (see `README.txt` inside). Windows SmartScreen will warn
that the game is unsigned.

**Known limits.** Windows only. Not code-signed. No controller support yet.
