## A mix defect the silent files had hidden

`AudioStreamPlayer3D` clamps `volume_db` plus distance gain at `max_db` (+3 dB). The footsteps
and Scout cues used the default 10 m unit size, so a footstep 1 m from the camera gained +20 dB
and every step hit the clamp: **`volume_db` on these nodes did nothing**. Measured directly, a
footstep at −8 dB and at −14 dB peaked identically (−6.9 dBFS); only at −26 dB did the level
fall. Nobody could hear this while the files were silent.

Fix: unit size 1 m for the player's own footsteps, 3 m for the Scout's cues. Regression test
`TestCloseCuesKeepTheirVolume` checks each close-range cue stays under the clamp at the distance
it is heard from. It fails on the old setting ("Footsteps_Metal reaches max_db at 1 m (12.0
dB)") and passes on the fix.

## Evidence

### Heard in play — `playthrough_journey.gd`

Each cue node is routed to its own bus with an `AudioEffectCapture`, so every mixed sample is
inspected; an onset is a 10 ms block above −60 dBFS after a block below it. Polling the bus peak
once a frame was tried first and missed most cues: the container renders at 3–4 frames per
wall-clock second, and the peak only reflects the latest mix buffer.

wall-clock time while the game here runs slower than real time, so two plays close together can
merge (Scout_Hurt read 6 in one run and 3 in another on the same route).

### Levels

The loudest ambient loop per walked leg ranges −30.3 to −23.4 dB (mean of per-frame peak).
Footstep peaks sit 3–18 dB above that and Scout cues 5–20 dB above. Volumes were set to
these measured targets (footsteps −8 dB, Scout alert −2, attack −9, hurt −10), **not by
listening**.

## Limits

- **No one has listened to these cues.** Their character (a plate ring, basalt grit, an ash
  shuffle, alien chirps and a screech) is what the generator is designed to produce, not a
  judgement that it sounds that way or sounds good. That belongs to human playtest note #1.
- Levels are set to measured peaks on the digital mix; a speaker or headphone check may move them.
- Each cue has one variant. Footsteps vary only by rotating three surfaces; the Scout repeats
  exactly.
- 19 silent files remain: weapon swing, impact and ready tone; four pickups; UI; save; state;
  Scout death. README §16's seven required cues are unaffected.

## Verdict

`PASS` for this item: the six triggered cues are authored, recorded, heard on the walked route
at measured levels, and a clamping defect that made their volume settings inert is fixed with a
regression test.
