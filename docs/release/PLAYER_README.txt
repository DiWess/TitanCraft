TITANCRAFT: CRASH SITE  -  version 1.0.0
=======================================

Your ship came down in the Mwezi Quarter, a coral-stone harbour quarter its
people left behind. The Galaxabrains - biomechanical things that strip every
machine for the parts that think - hold it now. The harbour beacon answered
your distress call once. Light it again.

A short single-player first-person game (about 20-40 minutes). Offline, no
account, no internet connection, no data collected.


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
1. Unzip the whole folder anywhere (keep the "data_TitanCraft..." folder next
   to TitanCraft.exe).
2. Run TitanCraft.exe.
   Windows SmartScreen may warn that the app is unrecognised, because the
   game is not code-signed: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".


CONTROLS (all keys can be changed in Settings)
--------
WASD / ZQSD ........ move              Shift ...... sprint
Mouse .............. look              Space ...... jump
E .................. interact, craft at the workbench, read notes
Left click ......... strike (Mechanical Arm Mk I)
Right click or F ... pull (Mk II magnet clamp)
Hold left click .... charge, release for a heavy strike (Mk III breaker coil)
Esc ................ pause, settings, save

The objective is always at the top left. Workbenches and save points repair
your suit; crafting and the power core are automatic checkpoints. If you fall,
"Reload Last Save" takes you back to your last checkpoint.


SETTINGS
--------
Mouse sensitivity, invert look, field of view, master volume, fullscreen,
VSync, reduced camera shake and key bindings are in Settings (main menu or
pause menu) and are saved on your computer.


REQUIREMENTS
------------
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, a graphics card with Vulkan support (most GPUs
from 2016 on). Target: 60 frames per second on a mid-range PC.


YOUR FILES
----------
Saves and settings live in:
  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\TitanCraft\
Delete crash_site_save.json there to start over, or choose New Game.
