## Project-authored content

- The Mwezi Quarter district, the Galaxabrain Scout, Swarm Drone and Warden,
  the mechanical arm modules, the barricade, gate, power core, story props and
  court paving were modelled for TitanCraft with scripted Blender generators.
- All sound effects and ambience loops were synthesised for TitanCraft from
  code (no samples or recordings).
- Terrain, sea and surface materials are procedural.
